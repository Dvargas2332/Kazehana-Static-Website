Our story

Kazehana started in a hotel lobby. Our founder — a former operations director — watched the front desk juggle five different systems just to check in a guest. Reservations in one tab, housekeeping in another, restaurant bookings on a paper clipboard.

We set out to build a single platform that connects every department. One login. One source of truth. No duct tape between tools. Today Kazehana serves over 200 properties across 14 countries, helping teams reduce check-in time by 60% and increase direct bookings by 35%.
