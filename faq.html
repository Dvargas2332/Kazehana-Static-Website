Frequently asked questions

How fast can we go live?

Most hotels launch in 3–6 weeks, depending on integrations and data migration.

Do you include training?

Yes. We deliver role-based training and stay with your team through the first month.

Can we import reservations and folios?

We import historical and future reservations, guest profiles, and folios on request.

Is support 24/7?

We offer extended support hours, with 24/7 options for multi‑property groups.

Do you offer memberships or plans?

Yes. We have memberships by package, and we help you choose the right tier based on your hotel size and needs.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use encryption at rest and in transit, maintain PCI DSS compliance, and follow GDPR standards for data privacy.

Which OTAs do you integrate with?

We connect with Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb, Agoda, and major regional OTAs through our channel manager.

Do you support multiple properties and languages?

Yes. Manage multiple properties from one dashboard. The interface supports English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Italian, and Japanese.

What hardware do we need?

None beyond a modern web browser. Kazehana runs on any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile. No local servers to maintain.

Can we cancel anytime?

We offer flexible terms. You can export your data at any time — no lock-in, no hidden fees.
